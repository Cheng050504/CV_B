Frequently asked questions

Is CV Builders free?

Yes. You can build and download your CV and cover letter without paying or creating an account.

Which template should I choose?

Use Finance for banking, consulting and private equity, Tech for software, product and data roles, and Generic for everything else. You can switch templates in the editor without losing what you've typed.

What file formats do I get?

You always get editable Word (.docx) files. When PDF export is available you'll also see PDF downloads; otherwise open the Word file and use Save as PDF.

Is my information saved?

Your draft is stored only in your own browser so you can come back to it. Documents are generated on the fly and are not kept on our server.

Can I make a CV with no work experience?

Yes. Lead with your education, then add clubs, volunteering, projects and part-time jobs in the experience step.

Do I get a cover letter too?

Yes. Step 5 asks a few questions about the role and fills in a cover letter that matches your CV template.
